## The bill

Impartner sells Impartner PRM on AWS Marketplace as a 12-month contract in four editions: Emerge US$25,000, Ignite US$45,000, Pro US$75,000 and Enterprise US$150,000. Ignite, Pro and Enterprise include up to 500 partner accounts with unlimited users, CRM integration and up to 2,000 API calls a month. Source: https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-l75vriw7iffmw (checked 2026-10-12). Implementation and added modules are quoted separately.
